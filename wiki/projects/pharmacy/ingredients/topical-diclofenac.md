# Topical Diclofenac

**Type:** Active Ingredient
**Drug Class:** Topical NSAID
**Evidence Grade:** Moderate-to-high — among the better-supported topical analgesics, especially for osteoarthritis/localized musculoskeletal pain
**Last updated:** 2026-08-23
**Sources:** raw/pharmacy-research/otc-guide-master-dossier-volume-2.md, raw/pharmacy-research/otc-evidence-corpus-chatgpt-continuation.md

## Indication
Osteoarthritis and localized musculoskeletal pain.

## Mechanism
Local NSAID action delivered topically, with the goal of achieving local tissue exposure while reducing systemic exposure compared to oral NSAIDs.

## Evidence Summary
Topical diclofenac is among the better-supported topical analgesics per the continuation source, particularly for osteoarthritis and localized musculoskeletal pain. Systematic reviews show clinically meaningful pain relief in osteoarthritis/acute musculoskeletal conditions, and diclofenac gel can outperform placebo. Adverse effects are more localized (application-site irritation, dermatitis) than with oral NSAIDs, but systemic NSAID exposure is not zero even with topical use.

## Pros
- Among the better-supported topical analgesics for osteoarthritis/localized musculoskeletal pain
- Systematic-review-level evidence of clinically meaningful pain relief
- Can outperform placebo in gel form
- Reduced systemic exposure compared to oral NSAIDs (goal of the topical route)

## Cons / Safety Limitations
- Application-site irritation, dermatitis
- Systemic NSAID exposure is not zero even with topical application — general NSAID cautions are not fully eliminated by the topical route

## Contraindications / Interactions
- Not reported in source material beyond the general NSAID caution above

## Special Population Flags
- Not reported in source material

## Formulation Caveat
The goal of the topical route is local tissue exposure with reduced systemic exposure versus oral NSAIDs — but this is a reduction, not an elimination, of systemic NSAID risk. Do not assume topical diclofenac is risk-free simply because it is applied to the skin.
