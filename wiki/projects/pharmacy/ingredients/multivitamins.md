# Multivitamins

**Type:** Active Ingredient (product class)
**Drug Class:** Multi-nutrient supplement
**Evidence Grade:** Strong rationale for nutrient-deficiency prevention; NOT established for chronic-disease prevention, mortality, cancer, or cognitive decline
**Last updated:** 2026-08-23
**Sources:** raw/pharmacy-research/otc-evidence-corpus-chatgpt-continuation.md

---

## Indication
Nutrient-deficiency prevention (strong rationale). Also marketed more broadly for chronic-disease prevention, which is a separate and much weaker evidence question.

## Mechanism
Not elaborated in source material.

## Evidence Summary
Evidence questions for multivitamins are separate and complicated: nutrient-deficiency prevention has a strong rationale, but chronic-disease prevention, mortality, cancer, and cognitive-decline benefits all require large, long-duration trials that the source does not describe as established. The source's central caution: "contains 100% DV" (Daily Value) on a label does not equal "prevents disease."

## Pros
- Strong rationale for preventing nutrient deficiency

## Cons / Safety Limitations
- Chronic-disease prevention, mortality reduction, cancer prevention, and cognitive-decline prevention are NOT established — these require large, long-duration trials
- Labeling showing "100% DV" for a nutrient does not equal a demonstrated disease-prevention benefit
