## Mechanism
Antiseptic action (not elaborated further in source material).

## Evidence Summary
Chlorhexidine is a very effective antiseptic mouthwash, but routine OTC-type chronic use is limited by tooth staining, taste disturbance, calculus (tartar) buildup, and mucosal effects. Per source, it is "more appropriate for targeted clinical indications than routine daily cosmetic use" — i.e., not intended as a general-purpose daily mouthwash the way a cosmetic rinse would be.

## Pros
- Very effective antiseptic action

## Cons / Safety Limitations
- Tooth staining
- Taste disturbance
- Calculus (tartar) buildup
- Mucosal effects
- These side effects specifically limit its suitability for routine, chronic, daily cosmetic use
