# Docusate

**Type:** Active Ingredient

## Indication
Marketed as a stool softener for constipation.

## Mechanism
Surfactant action intended to allow water and fat to penetrate and soften stool.

## Evidence Summary
**This is a deliberately preserved negative-evidence entry.** Despite being one of the most widely used constipation products, clinical studies have often failed to demonstrate substantial benefit over placebo or alternative approaches. This is exactly the kind of "high use, weak evidence" fact that's easy to lose if a database only tracks positive results.

## Pros
- Widely available, well-tolerated, low acute risk

## Cons / Safety Limitations
- Clinical trial evidence for meaningful efficacy is weak — studies have often failed to show substantial benefit over placebo or alternatives
- Should not be represented as a proven-effective constipation treatment
