# Benzoyl Peroxide

**Type:** Active Ingredient
**Drug Class:** Topical antimicrobial/keratolytic
**Evidence Grade:** High — strong clinical evidence for inflammatory acne
**Last updated:** 2026-08-23
**Sources:** raw/pharmacy-research/otc-guide-master-dossier-volume-2.md, raw/pharmacy-research/otc-evidence-corpus-chatgpt-continuation.md

## Indication
Acne (inflammatory).

## Mechanism
Antimicrobial activity against Cutibacterium acnes, plus keratolytic and anti-inflammatory effects.

## Evidence Summary
Strong clinical evidence for inflammatory acne (dossier), often used alone or with topical retinoids/antibiotics. The continuation source adds an important comparative safety point: bacterial resistance to benzoyl peroxide itself is not the same problem as antibiotic resistance, which is one reason it is often combined with topical retinoids or antibiotics (to help mitigate resistance concerns for the antibiotic component, per the general logic given).

## Pros
- Strong clinical evidence for reducing inflammatory acne lesions
- Bacterial resistance to benzoyl peroxide itself is not a significant concern (unlike antibiotic resistance)
- Often combined effectively with topical retinoids/antibiotics

## Cons / Safety Limitations
- Dryness, irritation, peeling
- Fabric/hair bleaching — a well-known practical downside

## Contraindications / Interactions
- Not reported in source material

## Special Population Flags
- Not reported in source material

## Formulation Caveat
Exact concentration and combination (with retinoids/antibiotics) matter (dossier's explicit caveat) — evidence for one concentration/combination should not be assumed to transfer to another.
