# Sore Throat Lozenges — Flurbiprofen

**Type:** Active Ingredient
**Drug Class:** NSAID throat lozenge (not just a demulcent)
**Evidence Grade:** Moderate-to-high — two positive RCTs with consistent, statistically significant results
**Last updated:** 2026-08-23
**Sources:** raw/pharmacy-research/otc-evidence-corpus-chatgpt-continuation.md

---

## Indication
Sore throat pain, difficulty swallowing, and throat swelling.

## Mechanism
Local NSAID-mediated anti-inflammatory/analgesic action — distinguished in source from AMC/DCBA lozenges, which act via local symptomatic analgesia rather than an anti-inflammatory mechanism. Neither treats the underlying viral infection.

## Evidence Summary
Two RCTs support flurbiprofen 8.75 mg lozenges for sore throat: the first found significantly greater reductions in sore-throat pain, difficulty swallowing, and swollen throat versus placebo within the first 24 hours; the second confirmed 8.75 mg significantly outperformed placebo for total pain relief, throat soreness, and swollen throat, detectable within 2 hours.

### Studies

**Schachtel BP, et al. — flurbiprofen 8.75 mg lozenges RCT**
- Citation: Flurbiprofen 8.75 mg lozenges for treating sore throat symptoms: RCT. PMID: 27734772, DOI: 10.2217/pmt-2015-0001
- Design: N=204 (102/102)
- Dosing: Up to 5 lozenges every 3–6 hours for up to 7 days
- Result (first 24h vs. placebo): Sore-throat pain 47% greater reduction; difficulty swallowing 66% greater reduction; swollen throat 40% greater reduction (all P<0.05)

**Second RCT**
- PMID: 27517548
- Design: N=320 (8.75 mg n=128, 12.5 mg n=64, placebo n=128)
- Result: 8.75 mg significantly outperformed placebo for total pain relief, throat soreness, and swollen throat, detectable within 2 hours

## Pros
- Two independent RCTs with statistically significant results (P<0.05) across multiple symptom domains (pain, swallowing difficulty, throat swelling)
- Effect detectable within 2 hours in the second RCT

## Cons / Safety Limitations
- Not reported in source material beyond the general NSAID mechanism (no lozenge-specific adverse-event data given)
- Does not treat the underlying viral infection

## Contraindications / Interactions
- Not reported in source material (general oral/topical NSAID cautions would be expected to apply but are not stated for the lozenge formulation specifically in source)

## Special Population Flags
- Not reported in source material

## Formulation Caveat
Flurbiprofen lozenges act via local NSAID-mediated anti-inflammatory/analgesic action, mechanistically distinct from AMC/DCBA lozenges' local symptomatic analgesia — see sore-throat-lozenges-amc-dcba.md. The two should not be treated as interchangeable or additive without direct evidence.
