# Lactase

**Type:** Active Ingredient
**Drug Class:** Digestive enzyme
**Evidence Grade:** Moderate-to-high — substantially stronger than many "digestive enzyme" supplements because it directly replaces the deficient enzyme
**Last updated:** 2026-08-23
**Sources:** raw/pharmacy-research/otc-guide-master-dossier-volume-2.md, raw/pharmacy-research/otc-evidence-corpus-chatgpt-continuation.md

## Indication
Lactose intolerance.

## Mechanism
Provides exogenous lactase enzyme to digest lactose that the body cannot otherwise break down.

## Evidence Summary
Controlled studies support reducing lactose-related symptoms when taken with lactose-containing foods (dossier). The continuation source adds an important comparative point: lactase's evidence is substantially stronger than many other "digestive enzyme" supplements precisely because it directly replaces the deficient enzyme, rather than relying on a more speculative mechanism. Effect depends on dose and lactose load.

## Pros
- Directly replaces the deficient enzyme — a more mechanistically direct rationale than most "digestive enzyme" supplements
- Controlled studies support symptom reduction when taken with lactose-containing foods

## Cons / Safety Limitations
- Generally well tolerated
- Effectiveness depends on dose and lactose load — not a guaranteed complete symptom elimination at any dose

## Contraindications / Interactions
- Not reported in source material

## Special Population Flags
- Not reported in source material

## Formulation Caveat
Evidence applies to lactase enzyme preparations specifically (dossier) — should not be generalized to other "digestive enzyme" blends that lack the same direct deficient-enzyme-replacement rationale.
