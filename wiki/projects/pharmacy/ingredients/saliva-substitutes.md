## Indication
Dry mouth (xerostomia) symptom relief.

## Mechanism
Not elaborated pharmacologically beyond lubrication/substitution or stimulation of saliva-like function.

## Evidence Summary
Ingredients include carboxymethylcellulose, glycerin, mucin-like formulations, and saliva stimulants. Benefits are primarily symptomatic. Per source, the underlying xerostomia cause matters — chronic dry mouth can increase caries, oral infection, dysphagia, and oral discomfort, and warrants evaluation for medication effects or systemic causes rather than being treated solely with a saliva substitute indefinitely.

## Pros
- Provides symptomatic relief of dry-mouth discomfort

## Cons / Safety Limitations
- Symptomatic only — does not address the underlying cause of xerostomia
- Chronic dry mouth carries real downstream risks (increased caries, oral infection, dysphagia, oral discomfort) that a saliva substitute does not resolve
- Warrants evaluation for medication effects or systemic causes, especially if chronic
