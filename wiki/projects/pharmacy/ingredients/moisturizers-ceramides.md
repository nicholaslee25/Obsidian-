# Moisturizers / Ceramides (Therapeutic Skincare)

**Type:** Active Ingredient (class)
**Drug Class:** Therapeutic moisturizer (ingredients: ceramides, petrolatum, glycerin, humectants)
**Evidence Grade:** Moderate-to-strong — moisturization itself has strong evidence in atopic dermatitis; brand recommendation share must not be read as an efficacy percentage
**Last updated:** 2026-08-23
**Sources:** raw/pharmacy-research/otc-evidence-corpus-chatgpt-continuation.md

---

## Indication
Atopic dermatitis / general therapeutic skincare and dry-skin management.

## Mechanism
Barrier restoration and hydration via ceramides, petrolatum, glycerin, and other humectants (mechanism not elaborated in more molecular detail in source material).

## Evidence Summary
Pharmacy Times 2026 lists CeraVe as the leading therapeutic-skincare recommendation at 32%. The source is explicit that this figure ("CeraVe 32%") does NOT mean "CeraVe improves eczema 32%" — it is a recommendation share, not a response rate. The relevant clinical evidence is for the ingredients themselves (ceramides, petrolatum, glycerin, humectants); moisturization as a category has strong evidence in atopic dermatitis.

## Pros
- Moisturization itself has strong evidence in atopic dermatitis
- Ceramides, petrolatum, and glycerin/humectants each have a plausible barrier-restoration/hydration rationale

## Cons / Safety Limitations
- Not reported in source material — no specific adverse-effect data given

## Contraindications / Interactions

## Special Population Flags
- Not reported in source material

## Formulation Caveat
The evidence is for the ingredients (ceramides, petrolatum, glycerin, humectants), not for any single branded product as a whole — a brand's recommendation share should not be read as validating its specific formulation's efficacy over another brand's.

## Pharmacy Times Context (recommendation share — NOT efficacy)
CeraVe — 32% recommendation for therapeutic skincare (Pharmacy Times 2026 OTC Guide, survey of ~1,700 pharmacists). Per source: "'CeraVe 32%' does NOT mean 'CeraVe improves eczema 32%.'"
