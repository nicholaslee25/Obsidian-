## Evidence Summary
Docosanol 10% is used OTC for herpes labialis; evidence suggests modest shortening of lesion duration when started early. The practical lesson per source: early application matters, and docosanol is not a cure for HSV (herpes simplex virus) — it shortens an outbreak, it does not eliminate the virus.

## Pros
- Modest shortening of cold-sore lesion duration when applied early

## Cons / Safety Limitations
- Effect is modest, not dramatic
- Not a cure for HSV — does not eliminate the underlying virus
- Benefit is time-sensitive: early application matters; the source does not describe benefit for lesions treated late in their course
