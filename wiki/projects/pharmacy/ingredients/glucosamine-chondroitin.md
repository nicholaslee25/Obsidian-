## Evidence Summary
The relevant condition is osteoarthritis. Outcomes that must be tracked separately include WOMAC pain score, physical function, stiffness, rescue-analgesic use, radiographic joint-space narrowing, need for surgery, and quality of life. The source's central caution: symptom improvement is not the same as structural disease modification — a product could plausibly reduce reported pain without demonstrably slowing joint-space narrowing, and these should not be conflated.
