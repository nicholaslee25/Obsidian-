# Terbinafine (Topical)

**Type:** Active Ingredient
**Drug Class:** Topical allylamine antifungal
**Evidence Grade:** High — particularly strong evidence base for dermatophyte infections, often with shorter treatment courses than azoles
**Last updated:** 2026-08-23
**Sources:** raw/pharmacy-research/otc-guide-master-dossier-volume-2.md, raw/pharmacy-research/otc-evidence-corpus-chatgpt-continuation.md

---

## Indication
Tinea pedis/cruris/corporis (athlete's foot, jock itch, ringworm).

## Mechanism
Inhibits squalene epoxidase, disrupting fungal cell-membrane synthesis.

## Evidence Summary
High-quality trials show high mycologic and clinical cure rates, often with shorter courses than some azoles (dossier). The continuation source adds a supporting meta-analysis: 19 RCTs / 2,899 patients found oral/topical terbinafine substantially better than placebo for mycologic cure. A direct comparative trial found terbinafine 1 week ~83% effective vs. clotrimazole 4 weeks ~82% (study-defined outcome) — terbinafine's practical advantage is the shorter course, not a dramatically higher cure rate (see clotrimazole.md).

### Studies

**Meta-analysis of oral/topical terbinafine vs. placebo**
- Design: Meta-analysis, 19 RCTs, 2,899 patients
- Result: Oral/topical terbinafine substantially better than placebo for mycologic cure.
- Note: No PMID/DOI given in source material for this specific meta-analysis.

**Terbinafine vs. clotrimazole comparative trial**
- Design: Comparative trial (tinea pedis)
- Result: Terbinafine 1 week ~83% effective vs. clotrimazole 4 weeks ~82% effective (study-defined outcome)
- Interpretation: Terbinafine's main practical advantage is often shorter treatment duration.

## Pros
- Strong evidence base for dermatophyte infections
- Rapid treatment, high cure rates
- Often shorter course than azole antifungals (e.g., clotrimazole)

## Cons / Safety Limitations
- Not appropriate for every fungal rash — diagnosis matters
- May irritate skin
- Local irritation possible (general topical-antifungal caution)

## Contraindications / Interactions
- Not reported in source material beyond the correct-diagnosis caution above

## Special Population Flags
- Not reported in source material

## Formulation Caveat
Among topical antifungals, terbinafine has a particularly strong evidence base for dermatophytes specifically — this should not be extrapolated to non-dermatophyte fungal or non-fungal rashes, where correct diagnosis is essential before treatment.
