# Vitamin C

**Type:** Active Ingredient
**Drug Class:** Vitamin/antioxidant supplement
**Evidence Grade:** Low for general prevention; possible modest benefit for cold duration/severity in specific circumstances (regular supplementation, extreme physical/cold stress) — not for after-onset use
**Last updated:** 2026-08-23
**Sources:** raw/pharmacy-research/otc-evidence-corpus-chatgpt-continuation.md

---

## Indication
Marketed for immune support and common-cold prevention/duration reduction.

## Mechanism
Not elaborated in source material beyond its role as an antioxidant/vitamin supplement.

## Evidence Summary
In the general population, routine vitamin C supplementation does not reliably prevent common colds. Regular (not after-onset) supplementation can modestly reduce cold duration/severity, and this effect is more apparent under extreme physical/cold stress (the source does not specify what constitutes "extreme physical/cold stress" beyond this general phrase). Starting supplementation after symptoms begin has not shown large, consistent benefit. A 2026 Pharmacy Times context note: liposomal vitamin C improves pharmacokinetic/pharmacodynamic (PK/PD) measures (i.e., better absorption), but translation to meaningful immune benefit remains inconclusive — improved absorption does not equal proven improved clinical outcomes.

## Pros
- Regular (prophylactic, not after-onset) supplementation may modestly reduce cold duration/severity, particularly under extreme physical/cold stress

## Cons / Safety Limitations
- Does not reliably prevent colds in the general population
- Starting after symptoms begin has not shown large, consistent benefit — this is a common real-world use pattern (people reach for vitamin C once they feel sick) that the evidence does not support
- Liposomal formulations improve absorption (PK/PD) but this has not been shown to translate into a proven immune-benefit improvement

## Contraindications / Interactions
- Not reported in source material

## Special Population Flags
- Not reported in source material

## Formulation Caveat
Liposomal vitamin C is a distinct formulation from standard vitamin C and should not be assumed to carry proven superior clinical benefit just because it has superior absorption — improved PK/PD is a surrogate measure, not a patient-important outcome, per the supplement-database rule established in this source ("a supplement can be biologically active without producing a clinically meaningful benefit").
