**Type:** Project
**Tags:** #project #electronics #clock #mechanical
**Last updated:** 2026-07-25

---

## Concept

A clock that displays time as 7-segment digits, but each segment is physically driven by a linear actuator instead of an LED — a mechanical/kinetic take on digital digits.

## Open Questions

- Actuator choice per segment: solenoid, small linear servo, or cam-driven push rod?
- 4 digits × 7 segments = 28 actuators minimum — cost and driver complexity at that scale
- Housing/material: wood face plate to hide the mechanism?
