## Knots to Learn

| Knot | Use |
|---|---|
| Bowline | Fixed loop that won't slip — wrap around stuff |
| Butterfly loop | Mid-line loop that lets you skip a damaged section of rope |
| Sheet bend | Join two ropes of different sizes/thicknesses |
| Trucker's hitch | Mechanical-advantage loop for cinching things down tight |
| Taut-line hitch | Adjustable loop that holds under tension (tent lines) |
